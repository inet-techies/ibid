 SECTION 1: INVITATION FOR PROPOSAL
1.1. PROPOSAL
 The Madhya Pradesh Police Telecommunication Department intends to procure
 various Information Technology (IT) and Surveillance Infrastructure
 components/equipment for the replacement and modernization of existing systems
 deployed under the Safe City Project implemented across 65 cities in the State of
 Madhya Pradesh.

 The objective of this procurement is to ensure the continued reliability, operational
 efficiency, and technological upgradation of the Surveillance and communication
 infrastructure supporting public safety, law enforcement, and smart policing initiatives.
 The selected bidder shall be responsible for the supply, delivery, and associated
 services for the specified equipment in accordance with the requirements of this RFP.

 The equipment shall be delivered to the Police Telecommunication Headquarters
 Store and/or any other location(s) within the State of Madhya Pradesh as designated
 by the Purchaser from time to time.

Core Deliverables and Responsibilities

 i. Supply the items as per BOQ and as the technical specifications and quantities
 specified by MP Police Telecommunication.
 ii. Ensure that all supplied equipment is new, genuine, unused, and sourced from
 authorized manufacturers or distributors.
 iii. Deliver the material MP Police telecommunication HQ, Bhopal warehouses, or other
 locations specified by MP Police Telecommunication across Madhya Pradesh.
 iv. Provide manufacturer warranty certificates, product datasheets, and relevant
 documentation for all supplied items.
 v. Ensure compliance with applicable quality standards, technical specifications, and
 regulatory requirements.
 vi. Replace any defective, damaged, or non-compliant equipment supplied during the
 warranty period, as per contract terms.
vii. Submit delivery challans, invoices, warranty documents, and other required supply-
 related records for acceptance and payment Processing.
viii. Coordinate with designated officials of MP Police Telecommunication for order
 execution, delivery scheduling, and material acceptance.
