                                     PROJECT OBJECTIVE

  1. PROJECT OBJECTIVE

     CCTV Surveillance Project with Night Vision Surveillance Camera & ANPR Camera Integration for
     Chicken Neck (Siliguri Corridor) & International Border Areas under IGP, North Bengal.

     The objective of the proposed project is to design, supply, install, commission, and operationalize
     an integrated, state-of-the-art CCTV surveillance system incorporating Automatic Number Plate
     Recognition (ANPR) cameras and high-resolution bullet cameras across the strategically sensitive
     Chicken Neck (Siliguri Corridor) region and adjoining international border areas under the
     jurisdiction of the Inspector General of Police (IGP), North Bengal.

     The project aims to enhance situational awareness, strengthen border and internal security,
     support intelligence-led policing, and enable real-time monitoring of vehicular and human
     movement across critical locations in the Police Districts of Darjeeling, Jalpaiguri, Cooch Behar,
     Alipurduar, Malda, Islampur, Raiganj, Dakshin Dinajpur and Siliguri Police Commissionerate.

  2. Specific Objective
  2.1 Strengthening Border and Internal Security
      •      To monitor vulnerable border stretches, transit routes, and infiltration-prone zones
      •      To deter and detect illegal cross-border activities including smuggling, trafficking, and
             unlawful migration
      •      To assist security forces in maintaining territorial integrity

  2.2 Intelligent Vehicular Monitoring through ANPR
      •       To automatically capture and recognize vehicle registration numbers in real time
      •       To create a searchable database of vehicle movement across districts
      •       To detect stolen, suspicious, or blacklisted vehicles
      •       To support enforcement of traffic regulations and investigation of criminal activities

  2.3 Comprehensive Area Surveillance
      •    To provide continuous visual coverage of strategic and high-risk locations including
           highways, junctions, entry/exit points, border approach roads, marketplaces, and public
           spaces
      •    To enhance monitoring during large gatherings, festivals, and emergencies

  2.4 Real-Time Monitoring and Rapid Response
      •      To enable centralized monitoring from designated District police control rooms
      •      To facilitate prompt decision-making and deployment of field units
      •      To improve emergency response capability

  2.5 Crime Prevention, Detection, and Investigation
      •     To deter criminal activities through visible surveillance
      •     To provide admissible digital evidence for investigation and prosecution
      •     To assist in post-incident analysis and reconstruction

  2.6 Inter-District Coordination
      •      To establish a unified surveillance network enabling seamless sharing of video feeds and
             data among multiple police districts
      •      To track movement of suspects across administrative boundaries

   2.7 Enhancement of Public Safety

Evaluation process

The tender shall be evaluated in 3 steps, as per the process set. As this tender shall be for selection of SI
partner and solution from OEMs as per the set technical specifications, the process shall be kept simple,
based on Eligibility, Technical Compliance and Price bid. The technically eligible bidders‟ price bids
shall be opened and the L1 bidder shall be awarded the contract. The following are key steps of the
tender evaluation process:

Tender Eligibility of Bidder – The bidders‟ eligibility to participate in the tender, based on the technical
experience, Project implementation experience, Services and Support infrastructure in West Bengal,
Financial capability shall be considered for eligibility. Additional eligibility criteria may be part of the
tender, based on the inputs of the tender committee. Bidders meeting the Eligibility criteria for the tender
shall be considered for technical evaluation.

Technical Evaluation – The technical evaluation shall be conducted by a technical sub-committee
appointed by the “Tender Committee”. The technical evaluation shall be based on the following broad
criteria:

# Adherence to the specifications for Active and Passive Components specified in the BOQ and technical
specifications of each equipment.

# Manufacturers‟ Authorization (MAF) for the bidder to supply the equipment from the OEM.

# OEM eligibility for the item quoted, as per the OEM eligibility terms and conditions specified in the
tender. All OEMs of primary equipment, such as IP CCTV, Software, Network Switch, Network Cables etc.
must meet the eligibility criteria set in the tender.

# The technical subcommittee shall evaluate each technical bid, and evaluate the technical compliance
for each item in the BoQ/BoM for the tender, and submit the same to Tender committee for final decision.

# All technically compliant bids shall be eligible for price bid opening.

The technically eligible bidders, based on technical evaluation and compliance summary shall be called
for “Price Bid” opening. The Price Bid of the L1 bidder, based on compliance to all priced items as per the
BoQ/BOM, shall be awarded the partnership contract for the project implementation.
