                                              SECTION – A
                                  OBJECTIVE OF THE PROJECT

The State Level Empowered Committee (SLEC) has decided that funds under the Modernization of Police
Force (MPF) Scheme earmarked for procurement of VMS Storage devices would be utilized for installation
of CCTV Cameras (including Audio & Night Vision) with 12 months local storage facility in Police Stations.

The Order dated 02.12.2020 of Hon’ble Supreme Court of India in SLP (Criminal) No. 3543/2020 in (Pramvir
Singh Saini V/s Baljit Singh & Others). The Hon’ble SC directed to all states and union territory governments
should ensure that CCTV cameras are installed in each and every police Stations functioning in the
respective State and/or union territory. Further, in order to ensure that no part of a Police Station is left
uncovered, it is imperative to ensure that CCTV cameras are installed at all entry and exit points, main gate
of police station, all lock-ups, all corridors, lobby, the reception area, all verandas / outhouses, inspector’s
room, sub-inspector’s room, areas outside the lock-ups room, station hall, in front of the police station
compound, outside wash room/ toilets, duty officers room, back part of the police station, child friendly
corner and any other important areas identified during site inspection and implementation of the project.
As per the plan submitted by WTL, the following units of the Kolkata Police shall be covered by 31st
December 2026.

67 (Sixty-seven) Police Stations to be covered by CCTV surveillance system under Kolkata Police with
Central Monitoring system for all CCTV surveillance under 91 PSs from 10 DRO and Kolkata Police HQ.

Kolkata Police authority unanimously decided that Webel Technology Ltd (WTL) will be given
responsibility for execution of this work (Phase -II Project) like previously executed Phase -I Project.

                                            SECTION – B
                                          SCOPE OF WORK

Kolkata Police Phase II Police Station CCTV Project & Centralised Monitoring System

The scope of work under Phase II of the Kolkata Police Station CCTV Project shall cover 67 (Sixty-Seven)
Police Stations identified for implementation by 31st December 2026, as per the requirements of Kolkata
Police and the approved budgetary provision.

The scope shall include Supply, Installation, Testing and Commissioning (SITC), integration and
comprehensive maintenance of CCTV surveillance systems, associated network infrastructure,
recording and storage systems, and Centralised Monitoring System at the identified locations.

The CCTV system shall provide facilities for real-time live viewing, recording, playback and retrieval
of video footage from the respective Police Stations. The system shall also facilitate authorised live
monitoring and playback from 10 (Ten) designated DRO Offices and the Central Control Room,
enabling centralised surveillance and monitoring by Kolkata Police.

1. CCTV Surveillance Coverage

CCTV cameras shall be installed at all identified Police Stations to ensure comprehensive coverage of
important, vulnerable and sensitive areas. The coverage shall include, but shall not be limited to:

      Entry and exit points of the Police Station building;

      Main gate and approach areas;

      Lock-ups and areas outside the lock-up rooms, subject to applicable rules and privacy
       requirements;

      Corridors, lobbies and reception areas;

      Verandas and outhouses;

      Inspector's and Sub-Inspector's rooms, wherever required;

      Station hall;

      Duty Officer's room;

      Areas in front of the Police Station compound;

      Areas outside washrooms/toilets;

      Rear and peripheral areas of the Police Station;

      Child Friendly Corner;

The actual location, type, number and orientation of cameras shall be finalised based on the detailed site
survey, site layout, security requirements and operational requirements of Kolkata Police.

2. Technical Scope

passive components, including but not limited to:

period.

and authorised export of recorded video footage.

The storage capacity shall be suitably designed considering the number of cameras, resolution, frame
rate, bitrate, compression technology, recording mode and required retention period.

4. Centralised Monitoring System

The Implementation Partner shall establish and integrate the CCTV systems with the Centralised
Monitoring System to enable authorised personnel to:

      View live CCTV feeds from the Police Stations;

      View live feeds at the designated DRO Offices and Central Control Room;

      Search and playback recorded footage;

      Retrieve and export recorded video;

      Monitor the health/status of cameras, NVRs, servers and network equipment;

      Manage users and access permissions;

      Monitor events and alarms, wherever applicable; and

      Maintain system logs and audit trails.

The proposed system shall be scalable and capable of accommodating additional CCTV cameras/sites in
the future, subject to the capacity of the proposed system architecture.

5. Site Survey and Installation

The Implementation Partner shall conduct a detailed site survey of all 67 Police Stations, 10 designated
DRO Offices and the Central Control Room, as applicable, before commencement of installation.

The survey shall assess the existing site infrastructure, camera coverage requirements, network
connectivity, bandwidth, power availability, equipment locations, cabling routes, racks, UPS
requirements, earthing and other associated requirements.

Based on the site survey, the Implementation Partner shall undertake complete installation, configuration,
integration, testing and commissioning of the system.

6. Logistics and Infrastructure

The Implementation Partner shall be responsible for transportation, delivery, unloading, installation and
commissioning of all equipment and materials required for the project.

The scope shall also include all necessary minor civil, electrical, cabling, mounting and associated works
required for successful implementation of the CCTV system, unless specifically excluded in the BOQ.

7. Integration

The Implementation Partner shall integrate the Police Station-level CCTV systems with the designated
DRO Offices and Central Control Room through the available network connectivity.

The integration shall ensure reliable transmission of live video and authorised access to recorded footage
without adversely affecting the recording and operational performance of the Police Station CCTV
system.
